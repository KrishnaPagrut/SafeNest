# Storylines (picked: A)

**A. The claim that had to prove itself** (recommended: the viewer follows one hazard check from phone to verdict, and the drop of a false claim is the hook).
- Device: the worked example. One walkthrough travels through three Meta models; one claim fails on camera. Form: the product's own screen, with the camera pushing into the part the voice names and the rest dimmed.
- First two seconds: the Review page mid-run, cards still pending, the SAM 3.1 step lit. A line lands: "One in four over 65 fall each year."
- Beats: hook on the live sort → upload → Spark names → SAM finds pixels → cards sort → a claim is dropped → mask tracks the rug → the missing grab bar goes to a person → Muse Image shows it fixed → re-film diff → one page → end card.
- Look: the app's own warm charcoal and Atkinson type; a single red reserved for SAM masks; dim-and-hole spotlight at 62% black.
- Sound: narrated, one calm piano bed under it, a soft pop when a card lands in a lane, a riser into the compare payoff.

**B. Before and after, split screen to one.** Left pane: the hallway as filmed; right: the same hallway after the weekend. Both play; the left collapses into the right as SAM confirms each fix. Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one. Opens on both panes already walking.

**C. Type carries the argument.** Giant kinetic numbers and phrases ("1 in 4", "0 px", "15 → 6") cut on the beat, with product screens only as punch-ins. Device: a number lands. Form: cut on the beat. Opens on "1 in 4" filling the frame.
